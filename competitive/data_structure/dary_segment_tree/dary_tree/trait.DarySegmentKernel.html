Expand description

Storage and ordered monoid operations for a d-ary segment tree.

B is a power of two greater than one. block receives at most B values and pads the remaining lanes with unit. combine(left, right) preserves order. A Change describes how a whole block’s aggregate changed: it may be a delta or its replacement value. Returning None from replace/update/repair stops propagation because that aggregate did not change; the block itself must still be updated.

§Safety

Unsafe operations receive in-bounds lanes/ranges (including empty ranges). ISA is 0 (portable), 1 (AVX2), or 2 (AVX-512F/DQ/CD/BW/VL), supported by the CPU. Implementations must provide the portable path on other architectures and check any additional instruction-set requirements themselves.
